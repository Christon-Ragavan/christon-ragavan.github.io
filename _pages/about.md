I'm Christon, a machine learning engineer and solution architect based in Munich. For more than eight years I've been building AI systems that leave the notebook and run in production, and leading the teams that build them.

## What I work on now

At [Cloudflight](https://www.cloudflight.io) I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer. Most of my time goes to aerospace: for an aircraft engine manufacturer I architected a live engine health monitoring platform on Databricks, and I now lead an AI-based failure diagnosis that checks which engine alerts actually matter.

Before that I worked across very different domains: clinical NLP and patient record consolidation for Siemens Healthineers, an enterprise knowledge-graph platform with private-cloud LLMs adopted by the German Federal Ministry of Education and BDO, a Whisper-based transcription service for NTU Singapore, and several generative AI proofs of concept. See [projects](/projects/) for details.

## How I got here

I studied Electronics & Media in India, then moved to Germany for a Master's in Media Technology at TU Ilmenau. I spent four years at the Fraunhofer Institute for Digital Media Technology doing deep learning research on audio and music: chord recognition, beat and periodicity estimation, source separation. I was product owner for two research technologies that went to industry clients, and I've [published](/cv/#publications) at ISMIR, SMC and ICMC.

Audio research taught me to care about time series, careful evaluation and baselines that are hard to beat. That still shapes how I approach sensor data, LLM systems and everything in between.

## What I'm good at

- Taking an AI use case from a workshop to a system people rely on
- Data platforms and MLOps on Databricks and Azure
- LLM applications: RAG, knowledge graphs, private deployments
- Time series and signal data, from audio to engine sensors
- Leading small teams and mentoring junior engineers
